# Wave 3Z2 — GCal 11 full-calendar independent evidence (2026-10-27)

## Verified source
The GCal-for-Windows maintainer documents the 11.x calendar phases for
Dvadasi, Ekadasi fasting and Parana. A third-party site publishes **GCal 11,
Build 5** full-year TXT exports by Indian city for 2026 and 2027:
- https://www.vaisnavacalendar.info/calendar-file-downloads/txt-calendar-files-2026
- https://www.vaisnavacalendar.info/calendar-file-downloads/txt-calendar-files-2027

The third-party website says its generated calendars use GCal, but is not the
GBC Calendar Committee itself. This is a *GCal program export*, not an
independently executed or compiled binary nor independent proof of any edition's
accuracy.

The separate research-only GitHub Action resolves fresh authenticated public
download URLs (WordPress links can include changing _wpnonce values), checks
published city and engine headings, loads all civil days, and compares all
published Ekadasi/Dvadasi fasts and Parana against annual sunrise-only
observations. It never publishes ritual outcomes into live SEO pages.
